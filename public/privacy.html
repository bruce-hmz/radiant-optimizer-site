Privacy

What Radiant Optimizer List does with information about you: no accounts, no game files, and how third-party services work.

Radiant Optimizer List is a static directory. There is no account, no comment box, and no newsletter. This page says what that means for information about you.

What we do not collect

  • We do not ask for your name, email, payment details or a password.
  • Banner placements on this site are served by third-party advertising networks, which may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver and measure ads — see the advertising section below.
  • Some ads come through Google AdSense, disclosed in the next section.
  • We do not sell or share your data with data brokers.
  • We do not receive game files. Projects that need data from a game you own ask for that data on their own sites, not here.

Analytics

This build of the site runs no visitor analytics or first-party statistics service.

Advertising

Ads, when shown, are served through Google AdSense. Third-party vendors including Google use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other websites, and to measure ad performance. You can review or change personalized-ad settings at Google Ads Settings and opt out of many third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info/choices. Where consent is required (for example in the EEA and UK) ad and analytics cookies are only set after you accept them in the site banner, and you can withdraw that choice any time by clearing this site's data. See also How Google uses cookies.

Banner placements are also served by independent advertising networks (scripts loaded from bauval.org). Those networks may use their own cookies or similar technologies to deliver and measure ads and operate under their own privacy policies. You can opt out of many third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info/choices.

What the host can see

The site is served through Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare can process connection data such as your IP address, the page you requested, and basic browser details, for delivery and security. That processing is covered by Cloudflare's privacy policy, not by a database we keep.

When you leave this site

Game pages, the official Radiant Optimizer site, posts on X, and the public repository are other people's services. Once you follow a link, their policies apply. We do not control what those sites log.

Corrections

If something on this page is wrong, or a link in the directory needs a correction, use the contact page.
