Browser games with no download

Every entry on this page starts without an installer and without you supplying game files. Open the link, press start, play.

These are the projects whose hosts list a browser start rather than a file upload. They are the fastest way to find out whether browser ports are for you. Be aware that "no download" does not mean "no data": several of these still transfer a large amount of game data on first load, and one entry here has an initial load of around 380 MB.

If you want the projects that expect your own game files instead, filter the full library by the "Bring your own files" badge.
