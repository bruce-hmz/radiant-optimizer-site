PES 6 Web Online

The PSP football classic rebuilt for the browser with pad and touch support.

Open the original project ↗ Back to directory

The project documents keyboard, gamepad and touch controls and responded on 9 Oct 2026.

What it is

This project identifies itself as the PSP version of PES 6 and documents keyboard, gamepad and touch controls. It is a browser rebuild rather than a cloud stream.

What you need

  • A desktop browser
  • A gamepad or keyboard for the best control
  • A stable connection for the initial asset load

How to open it

  1. Open the source destination from this page.
  2. Choose your input method on the setup screen.
  3. Kick off a friendly before starting a longer competition.
