OpenTyrian

A free, open-source vertical shooter with a full campaign.

Open project page ↗ Back to the original list

Verification status

Checked Oct 11, 2026; HTTP response 200. Gameplay not tested.

Project status / evidence: Open-source reimplementation of Tyrian with free assets, hosted by Decomp Games. Full campaign included.

What it is

OpenTyrian is an open-source reimplementation of Tyrian, the 1995 vertical scrolling shooter by Epic MegaGames. It keeps the full campaign and the ship-upgrade progression, and it ships with free assets rather than the original commercial ones. The result is a complete shooter rather than a demo level.

Requirement

No extra files listed No extra game files or installer are listed as requirements. This does not establish destination availability or gameplay.

What you need

  • A desktop browser
  • A keyboard or gamepad
  • Nothing else, since the assets are free and included

How to open it

  1. Open the OpenTyrian build on Decomp Games.
  2. Wait for the loader.
  3. Pick a difficulty and start the campaign, then spend upgrade points between levels.
