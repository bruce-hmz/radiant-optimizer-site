What it is

Quake II is id Software's 1997 shooter, the follow-up to the original, with a sci-fi campaign and a strong multiplayer scene of its own. The host describes multiplayer for up to eight players; a joined match was not tested, so treat the player count as the project's own claim.

What you need

  • A desktop browser
  • A keyboard and mouse
  • A stable connection, and enough players for a full lobby

How to open it

  1. Open the source destination from this page.
  2. Read the setup notes for controls and servers.
  3. Try a match with a few players before hosting a full lobby.
