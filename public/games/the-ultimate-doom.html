The Ultimate Doom

The expanded 1995 retail release, including the fourth episode.

Open the original project ↗ Back to directory

Runs the 1995 retail release, including the fourth episode, through browser-based DOS emulation.

What it is

The Ultimate Doom is the 1995 retail release that added a fourth episode, Thy Flesh Consumed, to the original three. It runs here through the same browser DOS environment as the 1993 build, so the difference between the two entries is the content, not the engine. Episode four is noticeably harder than the original campaign.

What you need

  • A desktop browser
  • A keyboard and mouse
  • Familiarity with classic Doom controls, since there is no tutorial

How to open it

  1. Open the Ultimate Doom page.
  2. Click to capture the mouse.
  3. Pick an episode from the menu; episode four is the hardest of the four.
