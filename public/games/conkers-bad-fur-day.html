Conker's Bad Fur Day

Rare's mature N64 platformer, rebuilt to run in a browser.

Open the original project ↗ Back to directory

Clean-room browser build of the N64 title, hosted by Decomp Games. Contains mature content.

What it is

Conker's Bad Fur Day is Rare's 2001 N64 platformer, and it is aimed squarely at adults: the humour is crude and the level design parodies films and other games. It also includes several distinct multiplayer modes alongside the campaign. This build runs from the community decompilation of the N64 original and carries the same mature content.

What you need

  • A desktop browser with WebGL
  • A gamepad or keyboard
  • An audience that is old enough for the content

How to open it

  1. Open the Conker build on Decomp Games.
  2. Wait for the loader.
  3. Check the controls before starting; the multiplayer modes are separate from the campaign.
