Minecraft Classic

Mojang's own free browser build of the original creative-mode Minecraft.

Open project page ↗ Back to the original list

Verification status

Checked Oct 11, 2026; HTTP response 200. Gameplay not tested.

Project status / evidence: Official Mojang build. Creative mode only, and multiplayer worlds vanish when the host closes the tab.

What it is

The official browser edition of Minecraft Classic, published by Mojang and free to play with no account. It is the original creative mode only, so there is no survival, no mobs and no crafting. Multiplayer worlds are peer-to-peer: they exist only while the person hosting keeps the tab open, which is why the player list is usually tiny.

Requirement

No extra files listed No extra game files or installer are listed as requirements. This does not establish destination availability or gameplay.

What you need

  • A desktop browser with WebGL
  • A keyboard and mouse
  • No account, launcher or purchase

How to open it

  1. Open the official Classic page at classic.minecraft.net.
  2. Enter a nickname when prompted, or leave it blank.
  3. Click the canvas to lock the pointer, then pick a block from the hotbar.
