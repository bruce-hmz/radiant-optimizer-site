Open Cadet

An open-source arcade game with free assets, playable in a browser.

Open the original project ↗ Back to directory

Smaller open-source arcade title with free assets, hosted by Decomp Games.

What it is

Open Cadet is a smaller open-source arcade title distributed with its own free assets, so nothing has to be supplied by the player. It is one of the lighter entries in this library and is included for completeness rather than as a headline title, so treat it as a short arcade diversion rather than a long campaign.

What you need

  • A desktop browser
  • A keyboard
  • Nothing else, since the assets are free and included

How to open it

  1. Open the Open Cadet build on Decomp Games.
  2. Wait for the loader.
  3. Check the controls on the title screen before starting.
