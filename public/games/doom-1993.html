Doom (1993)

The original Doom running from a real DOS executable inside the browser.

Open project page ↗ Back to the original list

Verification status

Checked Oct 11, 2026; HTTP response 200. Gameplay not tested.

Project status / evidence: Runs the genuine 1993 DOS executable through browser-based emulation, not a rewrite.

What it is

This entry runs the original 1993 Doom through a browser-based DOS environment rather than a JavaScript rewrite, so the genuine executable and its behaviour are preserved. That means the original renderer and sound handling, with the keyboard-only defaults of the era and mouse look added by the emulation layer.

Requirement

No extra files listed No extra game files or installer are listed as requirements. This does not establish destination availability or gameplay.

What you need

  • A desktop browser
  • A keyboard and mouse
  • Virtual touch controls if you are on a phone

How to open it

  1. Open the Doom page on DOS Zone.
  2. Click the screen to capture input.
  3. Press Escape for the in-game menu and key bindings.

Before you click
