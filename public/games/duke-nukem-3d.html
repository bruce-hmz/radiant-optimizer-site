Duke Nukem 3D

The 1996 shooter running from its DOS build in the browser.

Open project page ↗ Back to the original list

Project status / evidence: Runs the 1996 DOS build through browser emulation. Free to play without registration.

What it is

Duke Nukem 3D runs here from the 1996 DOS build through browser-based emulation, so the interactive scenery, the weapon set and the level design behave as they did on period hardware. It was one of the first shooters to build a genuinely city-like environment rather than corridors, and the build is the original one rather than a remake. The page is free to play without registration.

Requirement

No extra files listed No extra game files or installer are listed as requirements. This does not establish destination availability or gameplay.

What you need

  • A desktop browser
  • A keyboard and mouse
  • A click on the canvas to capture input

How to open it

  1. Open the Duke Nukem 3D page on DOS Zone.
  2. Let the DOS bundle finish loading, then click the screen to capture input.
  3. Use the in-game menu for controls, sound and difficulty.
