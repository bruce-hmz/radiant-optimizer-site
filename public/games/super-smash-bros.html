Super Smash Bros.

The original N64 party fighter, rebuilt for the browser.

Open project page ↗ Back to the original list

Verification status

Checked Oct 11, 2026; HTTP response 200. Gameplay not tested.

Project status / evidence: Clean-room browser build of the original N64 fighter, hosted by Decomp Games. Not affiliated with Nintendo.

What it is

Super Smash Bros. is HAL Laboratory's 1999 N64 platform fighter, the first in the series. It replaces health bars with an accumulating damage percentage, and it shipped with twelve characters and support for four players at once. This build runs from the community decompilation of the N64 original.

Requirement

No extra files listed No extra game files or installer are listed as requirements. This does not establish destination availability or gameplay.

What you need

  • A desktop browser with WebGL
  • Gamepads for two-player or four-player matches
  • A modern browser

How to open it

  1. Open the Super Smash Bros. build on Decomp Games.
  2. Wait for the loader.
  3. Check the control scheme, then pick a mode and characters before the match.
