Red Alert 2: Chrono Divide

Fan-made recreation of Red Alert 2. Independent project, not an official release.

What it is

Chrono Divide is a fan project that recreates Red Alert 2 for the browser. It is an independent build rather than an official release, and it targets the classic skirmish and multiplayer experience rather than the campaign. Expect the familiar factions and units behind a browser-native interface, with the project's own balance decisions.

What you need

  • A desktop browser
  • A mouse
  • A stable connection for multiplayer

How to open it

  1. Open the Chrono Divide site.
  2. Create an account or start a skirmish, depending on what the project currently offers.
  3. Read the project's own notes for controls and game modes.

Before you click
