Wacky Wheels

Apogee's animal kart racer, running from its DOS build in a browser.

Open the original project ↗ Back to directory

Runs the 1994 Apogee DOS build through browser emulation. The build boots as version 1.1 and reports itself as registered.

What it is

Wacky Wheels is Apogee's animal-themed kart racer, an answer to Super Mario Kart in which you collect hedgehogs and bombs and fire them at the other drivers. It runs here from the DOS build through a browser DOS environment, and it is notable for including a dedicated battle mode alongside the races, plus split-screen play in the original release. The build boots as version 1.1 and reports itself as the registered release, so the full character and course set is available rather than a cut-down demo.

What you need

  • A desktop browser
  • A keyboard, plus a mouse for the menus
  • Patience with DOS-era menus and key mapping

How to open it

  1. Open the Wacky Wheels page on DOS Zone.
  2. Click the screen to capture input.
  3. Pick a character and course, then drive with the arrow keys.
