Open Skyways

An arcade racer where you steer and jump a craft along narrow sky roads.

Open the original project ↗ Back to the original list

Clean-room engine reconstruction with its own thirty courses, hosted by Decomp Games. Audio is disabled.

What it is

Open Skyways rebuilds the engine behind SkyRoads, the 1993 arcade racer in which you steer a craft along narrow elevated roads and jump the gaps before you run out of road. It pairs that engine with thirty independently authored courses and replacement menus, dashboard and graphics, so none of Bluemoon's original data or music is included. Sound is permanently disabled in this build, and only the first road has been verified through its completion gate, so treat the other twenty-nine as untested.

What you need

  • A desktop browser
  • A keyboard, which this build requires
  • Awareness that the full thirty-road campaign is unverified

How to open it

  1. Open the Open Skyways build on Decomp Games.
  2. Press Enter to leave the intro, then choose Play and pick a road.
  3. Accelerate and brake with the up and down arrows, steer with left and right, and press Space to jump.
