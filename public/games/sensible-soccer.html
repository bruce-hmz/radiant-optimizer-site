International Sensible Soccer

The 1994 football classic, running from its DOS build in a browser.

Open the original project ↗ Back to directory

Runs the 1994 DOS build through browser emulation. Free to play without registration.

What it is

International Sensible Soccer is Sensible Software's 1994 football game, played from a zoomed-out top-down view that keeps passing and through balls readable at speed. It is remembered for tight controls and pace rather than presentation, and it runs here from the DOS build through a browser DOS environment with no registration. Virtual controls are provided for touch devices, though a keyboard is the better way to play it.

What you need

  • A desktop browser
  • A keyboard, or a gamepad if you prefer
  • Awareness that the DOS-era control scheme is not the modern default

How to open it

  1. Open the International Sensible Soccer page on DOS Zone.
  2. Click the screen to capture input.
  3. Pick a team and a match type from the menus, then check the key layout before kick-off.
